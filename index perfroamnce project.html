Your exact training structure depends on your individual needs. A typical Performance Project week may include:

  • 2 running / conditioning sessions
  • 1 additional off-legs conditioning session
  • Speed development
  • Power development
  • AFL or soccer-specific field work

  • VALD ForceDecks
  • VALD ForceFrame
  • Relevant strength measures
  • Jump / power measures
  • Asymmetry measures
  • 1RM bench press
  • Maximum pull-ups
  • 2km time trial

Not every athlete completes every assessment. Your testing battery is chosen around you.

Questions

Is Performance Project only for elite players?

No. It is designed for serious and aspiring elite AFL and soccer athletes. Your current level matters less than whether you are genuinely committed to improving.

Do I have to live in Adelaide?

No. Online athletes can complete the program interstate or overseas. Testing will be adapted around what is available to you.

Where does local testing happen?

Peaq Performance in Adelaide.

What app is the program delivered through?

TeamBuildr.

Is the program the same for every athlete?

No. Programming is individualised around your testing, sport, position, history, availability and goals.

What happens if my club schedule changes?

Your program can be reviewed and adjusted. This is coaching, not a static PDF.

What is the difference between Online and Hybrid?

Hybrid includes everything in Online plus one coached 90–120 minute group S&C session every week at Peaq Performance.

Is testing included?

Yes. Local athletes receive pre and post testing and individual reports. Combined standalone testing/reporting value is $440.

Is payment upfront?

The full investment is $997 for Online and $1,297 for Hybrid. If you would like to discuss payment options, note it on your application and we'll sort it out before you start.
